Will filling this out hurt my credit score?

No. Checking your options here won’t affect your credit score. A lender only runs a full credit check later, if you choose to move ahead with an application.

Are you a lender?

No. We’re a registered motor vehicle dealer. Financing is provided by third-party lenders, and every application is subject to lender review and approval. Rates and terms vary by lender and by applicant.

I’m self-employed. What will I need?

Usually proof of income such as recent bank statements or tax filings (like your Notice of Assessment). Your specialist will tell you exactly what the lender needs.

Do I need a down payment?

Down payment requirements vary by lender and by applicant, and are determined when your application is reviewed. Your specialist will go through the options available to you.

Am I obligated to go ahead?

Not at all. There’s no obligation and no cost to pre-qualify, and pre-qualifying is not an approval. Your information stays confidential.
