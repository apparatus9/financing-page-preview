Will filling this out hurt my credit score?

No. Pre-qualifying here is not a credit check. If you decide to move forward with a lender, they may run a credit check at that point — and we'll tell you before that happens.

Are you a lender?

No. We're a registered motor vehicle dealer. Financing is provided by third-party lenders, and every application is subject to lender review and approval. Rates and terms vary by lender and by applicant.

I'm self-employed. What will I need?

Usually proof of income such as recent bank statements or a notice of assessment, plus a valid licence. A specialist will tell you exactly what your lender needs before you gather anything.

Do I need a down payment?

Not always. A down payment can improve your approval odds and lower your payment, but there are options with little or nothing down, subject to lender approval.

Am I obligated to go ahead?

Not at all. Pre-qualifying shows you what's available. You decide whether to move forward, and there is no cost or obligation either way.
